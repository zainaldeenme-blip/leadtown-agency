Privacy policy.

LeadTown respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be shared when you contact us and how we use it.

Information you provide

If you start a growth audit or contact us through WhatsApp, you may provide your name, business name, contact details and information about your marketing goals.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand your business needs, prepare recommendations and provide services you request. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Our website may link to services such as WhatsApp. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them. Website hosting providers may process basic technical information required to deliver and secure the site.

Retention and access

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery and legal obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you.

Contact

For a privacy request, contact LeadTown through the WhatsApp link on our website.
