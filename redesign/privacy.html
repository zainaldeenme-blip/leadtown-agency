Privacy, in plain English.

This notice explains how information is handled on this LeadTown website preview.

Your quote brief

The quote builder prepares a message in your browser. The website does not store your form details on a server, and they are not automatically sent to LeadTown. Closing the builder keeps your draft only within the current page. Reloading clears it.

If you choose to continue to WhatsApp, the details in your brief are passed to WhatsApp as part of a pre-filled message. You still need to press Send there. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own privacy policy applies. You may instead copy the brief and share it through a channel you choose. Avoid including confidential, financial, medical or other sensitive information in the brief.

Responding to an enquiry

If you send the brief, LeadTown uses the details you provide—such as your name, business, email and project requirements—to respond, clarify your needs and prepare a quote. We do not sell your personal information.

Technical services

GitHub Pages hosts this preview and may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the website. Google Fonts serves the website font and may receive technical request data such as your IP address. The 3D scene is rendered locally in your browser. No advertising pixels, analytics scripts or marketing cookies have been added to this preview.

Choices and contact

Sound starts off and only plays when enabled. You can pause motion; your device’s reduced-motion setting is respected. These settings are not saved across visits. To ask about information you have sent, request access or correction, or discuss deletion, contact LeadTown through WhatsApp. The right retention period depends on the enquiry, any subsequent service relationship and applicable obligations.
