04 / A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Glad you asked.

Do I need the full system?+

No. Start with a website, Meta ads, Instagram management or lead tracking. We’ll discuss whether anything else is needed and quote only for the agreed scope.

What does it cost?+

Each project is quoted individually. The brief, deliverables, complexity and ongoing support determine the price. Any ad spend or third-party costs are separated before you approve.

Can you work with what I already have?+

Yes, we can review your existing setup first. What can be retained depends on platform access, the condition of the build and what the project needs to achieve.

How long will the work take?+

We confirm a proposed timeline after understanding the scope and what access or content is needed. You’ll know the milestones and approval points before work begins.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?+

No. Outcomes depend on your market, offer, budget and follow-up as well as the work itself. We agree useful measures and report honestly rather than promising a number.

What happens after I request a quote?+

The quote builder prepares your brief. You can review and send it through WhatsApp to start a conversation. We clarify the details before preparing a written quote. It doesn’t book a meeting or commit you to a purchase.
